Help — permissions, observations and task work

Your farm list comes from saved memberships, not a frontend role value. Viewer reads only. Owner/editor may add fields, observations, approve tasks, record actual work and follow-up evidence in their assigned farms. Only a platform administrator may create a new farm here; the creator receives its owner membership in the same transaction.

An observation requires an existing field and the facts you actually observed. Category classifies the record; source is user-reported, not an authenticated qualification. Blank time uses submission time. Explicit local times are converted to timestamps with an offset. Missing soil moisture remains unknown; zero means a measured zero and requires its method.

Saving an observation creates its review task and original snapshot atomically. Approval records human review, not a diagnosis or action. Record work only after it was performed and after approval. A later follow-up describes reported evidence; no causation or effectiveness is inferred. Existing chronology and state checks still apply.

Fictional format example: “DEMO: Some leaves were yellow; counts unknown.” Do not save examples as farm evidence. Use facts and uncertainty. Refreshed lists show bounded recent records, not complete history. No record export or attachment route is enabled in this stage.

No farm assigned: ask your operator. Permission changed: reload the selection. Session expired or account disabled: sign in again or contact your operator. If saving fails, refresh records before retrying; unsaved text is not autosaved. Changing farm discards drafts only after confirmation.

Unsupported sections (inspections, decisions, resources, market, finance, photos/audio, exports) are unavailable in this authenticated server. The separate legacy launcher remains single-user and must not be exposed to other users. Keep this loopback development workspace local; shared hosting and speech/chatbot expansion remain deferred.
