Help — seasons, dates and permissions

Choose an assigned farm. Owners and editors can save seasons and record season context for a field observation. Viewers can read. An inspection follows its parent observation; season context is saved on that observation.

Dates record the stated plan or planting record; they do not establish growth stage. Choose Unknown season when you cannot establish a link. No context recorded means nobody has saved an entry. Unavailable means a prior link is no longer valid or cannot be read.

For a new season, select the correct field, then enter a name, variety, area in hectares and dates. Fictional example: DEMO rainy season; DEMO variety; 0.5 hectares. The expected harvest must be later than planting. An existing season stays on its original field. Editing it does not rewrite captured context.

If records changed or access expired, refresh and review before retrying. Save results may be unknown after a connection drop; check the list before creating the same season again. This page does not offer deletes, closure, exports or harvest editing.
