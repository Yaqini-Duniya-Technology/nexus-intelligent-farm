        Help — sign-in, farm permissions and notes

        An operator creates your account and assigns an existing farm in the administration CLI. Usernames and passwords are never prefilled. Forgotten passwords require operator support; password reset is not implemented yet.

        The farm selector lists only farms granted to your account. Viewer can read; editor and owner can read and add notes. Grants are checked again at save time. Owner does not mean that this pilot provides account-management pages.

        Use notes for supported facts and uncertainty. Fictional example: “DEMO: Inspection date has not been confirmed.” Replace it with your actual information; do not save examples as evidence. Saving a note creates a separate pilot record, not an observation, task, diagnosis or payment. No note is autosaved.

        The session expires after 30 minutes without an authenticated request or after eight hours overall. Sign out on shared computers. Five failed sign-ins within 15 minutes temporarily limit that username. Refresh if permission changed.

        This is a loopback HTTP development pilot. Cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite Strict; a Secure cookie and TLS deployment configuration remain prerequisites for hosting. Legacy exports, attachments and forms are deliberately unavailable in this pilot because their account permissions are not integrated yet.
