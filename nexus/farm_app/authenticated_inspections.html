Help — permissions, inspection steps and corrections

Use this page to record an inspection actually performed for a saved observation. Select your assigned farm, then an observation. Viewers can read; owners and editors can save. Membership is checked again when reading or saving. A farm owner is not automatically a qualified adviser.

  1. Select the observation and check its field and text.
  2. Choose New inspection for another visit, or an uncorrected entry when fixing a previous record.
  3. Enter the actual inspection time, reporter, inspected area, selection method, affected-plant definition, findings and reason.
  4. Enter counts only when recorded; save and check the history.

Fictional example: DEMO — 10 plants checked, 2 meeting a stated definition. This describes that sample only; it does not establish whole-field prevalence or a cause. Never save examples as actual evidence.

Blank counts mean unknown; zero means you counted none. Affected plants cannot exceed plants checked. Plants and cobs are separate units. Optional cob grouping requires all three groups, including zero, summing to cobs checked. A cob belongs to one group only. Record uncertainty rather than guessing maturity.

Corrections append a new entry and retain the earlier one. Use a specific reason explaining what changed. New visits should be new inspections. Saving does not complete workspace tasks or create decision actions.

No assigned farm: contact your operator. Permission denied: refresh membership or contact your operator. Changed observation/history: refresh records and review the latest entry before accepting the new history or choosing another correction target. Refresh never converts a correction into a new visit. Validation errors keep your text. If the save response is lost, use Retry same save before changing the draft.

On session expiry your form remains in this tab; sign in through Account and notes in another tab, then use Refresh records here before retrying. Unsaved text is held only in this page's memory and is lost if you close the tab or reload the browser page. Use Refresh records to keep it. Do not leave private text on shared devices.
